# Coffee Sales Analysis — Excel Dashboard

## About the Project

I built this Excel dashboard to explore coffee sales across different coffee types, roast preferences, countries and customers. The workbook contains three connected tables: Orders, Customers and Products.

Rather than looking at the sales records individually, I wanted to bring the information together in a dashboard where I could compare product performance, identify valuable customers and explore how sales changed over time.

## What I Found

* **Light-roast Excelsa stands out in the roast comparison.** Its chart value is approximately 2,280, the highest among the coffee type and roast combinations shown. Light-roast Arabica is also relatively strong at around 2,000.

* **Medium-roast Arabica performs well compared with other medium roasts.** It reaches approximately 2,130, slightly above medium-roast Excelsa at around 2,100. Both are noticeably higher than medium-roast Liberica and Robusta.

* **Robusta has the lowest values across all three roast categories.** Its bars sit at approximately 1,570 for Dark, 1,280 for Light and 1,300 for Medium. This is a consistent pattern in the product comparison, although the chart alone does not explain the reason.

* **The United States dominates the monthly trend shown in the dashboard.** Its plotted values are generally much higher than those for the United Kingdom and Ireland, with several peaks above 800 and one reaching roughly 1,080. The other two countries have smaller, more variable monthly values.

* **The top-customer chart shows a clear gap between the leading customer and the next few customers.** Brenn Dundridge has the longest bar at approximately 310, followed by Brice Romera at around 250. The other three customers shown are clustered near 205–210.

These comparisons helped me look beyond overall sales and explore differences between products, markets and individual customers.

## What I Built

* A product comparison across coffee types and roast levels.
* A top-five customer chart.
* A monthly trend chart comparing countries from 2019 to 2022.
* Interactive slicers for coffee type, year and loyalty-card status.
